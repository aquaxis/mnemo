# Mnemosyne

In Greek mythology, **Mnemosyne** is the goddess of memory and remembrance, and
the mother of the nine Muses. Her name is the root of the word *mnemonic*.

Mnemo takes its name and concept from her: an omnipotent archive of memory and
knowledge.
