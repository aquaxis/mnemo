# Welcome to Mnemo

**Mnemo** is your integrated, Markdown-based knowledge archive — named after
**Mnemosyne**, the Greek goddess of memory.

## What you can do

- **Write notes** in Markdown with a live editor (left = source, right = preview).
- **Organize** notes into categories in the left pane.
- **Collect** knowledge from the web in the *News Feed* view.
- **Schedule** recurring collection with cron in the *Scheduler* view.

## Tips

- Everything is stored as plain Markdown under `data/notes/<category>/`.
- Binary files live separately under `data/assets/`.
- Use the search box to find anything across your archive.

Happy archiving!
